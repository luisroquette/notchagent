04 / TRUST MODEL

Your credentials
do not become our data.

NotchAgent has no hosted backend and no telemetry. Credentials stay in the macOS Keychain, account sessions use isolated WebKit profiles, and exported diagnostics remove credentials, identities and financial amounts.

Keychain storageOpt-in accountsSanitized diagnosticsOpen source
The optional Claude quota probe can make a paid one-token request and can be disabled in Settings.
